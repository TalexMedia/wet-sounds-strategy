02 Three Concepts For Wet Sounds

Three Opportunities to Grow Your Audience

One flagship documentary series, one short run of real tests, and one makeover series. Each one names the format, the show it borrows from, and a company that has already made it work. We film and produce at every level, so where you start is up to you.

How Wet Sounds could use it

The same format could be built around the people who install your gear, or the riders and boaters who live with it every weekend. Each episode would stay with one person for a full day on the water or the trail, and the value is that your gear earns its place in the background instead of being explained.

Why long-form episodic wins over time
One-off videos are not appointment content. A returning series gives people a reason to come back for the next person's story.
Trust builds in small moments repeated over time, and every episode is another chance to earn it.
Start with story, follow with facts. One person's account tells the audience more than a page of claims ever could.

How Wet Sounds could use it

This would employ a similar format, where each episode takes one claim you already make and tests it for real. That could be a tower speaker in the spray behind a wake boat, or a Harley kit ridden through a rainstorm. The value is that a buyer gets the answer they were already looking for, without anyone having to make the claim again.

Why a short run of real tests works
Entertainment is value given first. Nobody passes around a spec sheet, but people watch a claim get tested for real.
Answer the question they already have. A buyer is trying to work out whether the gear survives the conditions, and a test answers that better than a paragraph can.
The world resets every episode. There is another product and another condition to test, so the series never runs out.

How Wet Sounds could use it

The same format could be built around a boat that has never sounded right, or a UTV going in for its first upgrade. Each episode would end on the reveal and the first time the owner hears the system, and the value is that every install becomes a short story with an ending worth waiting for.

The world resets every episode. There is always another boat, another rig and another owner, so the series never runs out.
03 Lead With Value

The Legendeering Framework

The methodology behind every Talex production, and behind the three concepts above. Five directives, one core principle.

Legendeering is Talex's proprietary strategy. The core principle is straightforward: lead with value, always. The brand earns trust by giving without expectation, and that trust grows over time because people keep choosing to watch.

Five directives drive it:

  • Give Without Expectation. Value delivered without strings. The kind of generosity that builds loyalty money cannot buy.
  • Be Relentlessly Authentic. Say true things. Show real work. Speak like a human being.
  • It's Not About You. It's About Them. The audience has to come first. It's all about their wants and needs and making something they will choose to watch.
  • Start With Story, Follow With Facts. Story gives information shape, context, and emotional weight. Facts alone rarely move people.
  • Consistency Is Key. Trust is built in moments, repeated over time.

Wet Sounds is expert at building audio that keeps working in water, sun and vibration. Talex would add the video: a documentary series, a run of real tests, and a makeover series, each one built on the method above. The three concepts came straight out of it.
